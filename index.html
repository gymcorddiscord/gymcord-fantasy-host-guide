Fantasy League Host Setup Checklist

Step-by-step setup for hosting a fantasy league in Discord.

⚠️

NOTE: It is best to do this on a desktop computer. Please read all instructions and look at screenshots CAREFULLY. Ask questions in #fantasy-hosts-training AFTER reading all the instructions here.

Step 1

Determine Your Rules & Announcement

Use the wizard below to answer a few questions about your league. It builds a ready-to-post, Discord-formatted announcement for you:

League Rules Wizard ↗ Open in new tab
Step 2

Create the League Role

💡

You only need to do this once per League.

  1. Go to #bot-commands.
  2. Type: /role create
  3. Enter your League's name, ensuring it includes your Gymcord username or display name in the name of the league, e.g. Christa's Theme Chaos.
Step 3

Create the League Channel

  1. Under Fantasy Leagues category, create a new text channel with your league name.
⚠️

IMPORTANT: Make sure the toggle is set to PRIVATE.

  1. When it asks you which roles can have access to this channel, select the Role that you just created.
⚠️

Important: Go to the channel that you just created and add the rules to the channel and pin them.

Step 4

Set Up the Reaction Role

  1. In #bot-commands, type: /reactionrole setup
  2. Follow the prompts and link it to the league role you just made.
  3. It will ask you which channel the announcement should be in. To test your announcement, respond #fantasy-test. For the real deal, use #fantasy.
⚠️

IMPORTANT: You may only post to #fantasy if you have received a ✅ from Christa that your training is complete.

  1. It will ask you to include the title and description. Paste the title and description from the wizard in Step 1. If you take too long you will have to restart.
  2. Next it will ask you if you want to add a color to your announcement. Type in a hex code or type none.
  3. Then it will ask you to include the emoji for the reaction and the role that you created. Add it like this:
  1. Once a green checkbox appears, type done.
🚨

PLEASE READ - DO NOT SKIP

As the host, you DO NOT need to react to your own post to be in the league. The bot reaction is a stand-in for your spot in the league, so 8 reactions total = 8 people in the league.

IMPORTANT: Do not post to #fantasy until you've received a ✅ from Christa that your training is complete. Until then you must post to #fantasy-test.

IMPORTANT: Go to the channel where your announcement is posted and ensure that it was posted correctly.

If you are announcing your league for real (after training) comment with the tag @Fantasy to ping potential players.

Step 5

Remove the Announcement When Full (for #fantasy posts)

  1. Once the league is full, screenshot the list of names who reacted to the post for record keeping purposes.
  1. To get a roster of who was assigned the role to your league, type !dump @[League Role] into your league channel or #bot-commands.
  2. If any people are missing from the !dump list who reacted to your post, you can add them with the command /role add.
  3. Once your league is filled, please delete the announcement from the #fantasy channel AND delete your message that pinged @Fantasy.
Step 6

Announce Your Draft Order

Your players will be looking forward to hearing from you quickly!

We recommend using random.org/lists or the /shuffle command to randomize names.

Happy drafting! 🤸

Channel Usage

  • Please only put bot commands in #bot-commands. (Conversations aren't meant to be there.)
  • Please use the #fantasy-hosts-training channel for questions about this process, including tagging Christa to review your test post.
  • You may send test posts to #fantasy-test at any time from #bot-commands if you want to test the look and feel of your post after training.
